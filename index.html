Privacy Policy — Neon Blocks

Effective date: July 23, 2026

Neon Blocks ("the app") is a free block-puzzle game published by Strife Games. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or contact details. Your scores, coins, streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded by us.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, and coarse device data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in your device's Google settings. Purchasing the one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade disables all ads.

In-app purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details.

Leaderboards (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your gamer profile and scores are handled by Google Play Games Services under Google's Privacy Policy. Playing without signing in keeps all scores local.

Analytics

The app may use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics (e.g. session length, feature usage) that help us improve the game. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Notifications

Optional daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device, and you can turn them off any time in Settings or in your system notification settings.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are configured through Google AdMob's standard tools.

Data deletion

All game data is stored on your device: uninstalling the app deletes it. To remove ad-related data held by Google, use the controls linked above.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address. Questions: millstrife@hotmail.com.
